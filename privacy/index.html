Privacy Policy – Ardèche Gorge Guide

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Ardèche Gorge Guide app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is provided by the Ardèche Gorge Guide team ("we", "us"). Contact: Tomwahiizz@gmail.com.

The app does not require an account, does not show ads and does not sell your data.

1. Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show your position on the river map, your progress along the route and what's coming up next. Your live location is processed only on your device and is not sent to us or stored on our servers. You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings; the map, markers and safety information still work without it.

2. Community photos, videos and comments

You can choose to share photos or videos of river spots, add a caption, add comments and like or report photos. When you do, we store:

  • the photo or video, and any caption or comment you write;
  • the display name you enter (optional – if you leave it empty you are shown as "Anonymous");
  • information read from the photo file, such as the date it was taken and, if your camera recorded it, the GPS location where the photo was taken;
  • a random identifier created by the app on your device, used to show which posts, likes and reports are yours. It is not linked to your name, phone number or advertising ID.

Shared photos, videos, captions, display names and comments are public: other users of the app can see them. Please don't share anything you want to keep private. Shared photos may be analysed automatically (see section 3) to add context about the spot.

3. Nature ID (wildlife identification)

If you use Nature ID, the photo you take or choose is sent to an artificial-intelligence service to identify the plant or animal. The photo and the identification result are saved to a public wildlife sightings record for the Ardèche Gorge. No location other than "Ardèche Gorge" is attached to these sightings.

4. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) so we can send river and safety alerts. This uses a technical device token; we don't use it to identify you. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

5. Information stored only on your device

Your language choice, the lessons you have completed or saved, and your app identifier are stored on your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

6. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase – storage of shared photos, videos, comments and wildlife sightings;
  • Google (Gemini AI, through the Lovable AI gateway) – identifying wildlife and analysing shared photos;
  • Google Firebase – push notifications;
  • Map tile providers – loading map imagery. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address when map images are downloaded.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. How long we keep data

Shared photos, videos, comments and sightings are kept until they are removed by us or you ask us to delete them. Content that breaks our rules may be hidden or removed.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to delete content you have shared, or ask about the data we hold, by emailing Tomwahiizz@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, please tell us which spot and roughly when you posted, so we can find it. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have rights under data protection law (GDPR), including the right to access, correct or delete your data and to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
